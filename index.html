Features

  • Electronic Lab Journal - Design and keep track of all your synthesis experiments.
  • Integrated Web Search - Search for compounds in online databases.
  • Automatic Reaction Scheme - Automatically draws a reaction scheme based on the selected components.
  • Drawing tool - Draw new compounds in the sketcher.
  • Portable - No installation required.

About the Project
