Features

  • Electronic Lab Journal – Design and keep track of your synthesis experiments.
  • Integrated Web Search – Search for compounds in online databases.
  • Automatic Reaction Scheme – Automatically draw a reaction scheme based on the selected components.
  • Drawing Tool – Draw new compounds directly in the sketcher.
  • Portable – No installation required.

About the Project

Hello, I am a former PhD student in organic chemistry. I created this app as an easy option to set up and document your synthesis experiments. Feel free to try it out and let me know what you think!
